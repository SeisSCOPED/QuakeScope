How it was made

PickBlue obs weights (PhaseNet architecture, SeisBench 0.12.5), P and S thresholds 0.2; the short-period EL band is picked for OBS only. Picks are written per shard as Parquet partitioned by network, year and month; each shard writes a manifest; since image d0ccf9b every planned station-day also gets an outcome. The catalogue was built by these queues, all writing into the same prefix:

Gaps

NV (Ocean Networks Canada), refused to our account; network-years absent from EarthScope's S3 archive (most obs `not_found`); 18,832 station-days in short gaps.

Cost

Estimated, not billed: compute hours priced at $0.0213 per vCPU-hour, a rate calibrated on CloudBank's billed costs for three days of full load. The account cannot read AWS cost APIs. Storage of this catalogue is about $0.07 a month in S3 Standard; public downloads cost about $0.09 per GB once the account's free tier is used.
